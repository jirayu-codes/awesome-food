# awesome-food
### 🍳 Getting Started

- [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com) - A food-science-forward cooking site with deeply tested recipes, technique breakdowns, and guides that explain *why* a method works, not just how.
- [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com) - The largest community-driven recipe platform, with millions of user-submitted dishes, ratings, reviews, and built-in meal planning tools.
- [Food52](https://food52.com) - A one-stop cooking community combining curated recipes, editorial food content, and a shop, with standout video series from respected chefs.
- [BBC Good Food](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com) - A comprehensive recipe hub covering every cuisine, skill level, and dietary need, with user ratings and video tutorials from professional chefs.
- [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com) - A daily food magazine offering recipes alongside practical cooking tips, technique explainers, and deep-dive guides for home cooks of all levels.

### 🔧 Advanced Tools & Data

- [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov) - The US government's free, authoritative nutrition database with a public REST API covering 300,000+ foods — the gold standard for food data in research and app development.
- [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) - An open-source, crowd-sourced database of 2.8 million food products from 150+ countries, complete with a free API, Nutri-Score, and NOVA processing ratings.
- [Spoonacular API](https://spoonacular.com/food-api) - A developer-friendly food API with endpoints for recipe search, ingredient parsing, nutrition analysis, and meal planning, well-suited for building food apps.
- [Edamam](https://developer.edamam.com) - A nutrition analysis and recipe API that supports ingredient parsing, diet and allergen filtering, and structured meal data for health-focused applications.
- [recipe-scrapers (GitHub)](https://github.com/hhursev/recipe-scrapers) - A Python library that extracts structured recipe data from hundreds of recipe websites, invaluable for building recipe aggregators or food datasets.
